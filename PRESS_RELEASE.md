# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**Team Agnivega Unveils Smart Krishi-Yatra AI to Maximize Farmer Realization Through Advanced Logistics**

**[City, Date]** — Team Agnivega today announced the launch of the Smart Krishi-Yatra AI prototype at SKH041, a breakthrough agricultural logistics operating system designed to answer the most critical question facing modern farmers: _Where, when, and how should produce be transported to maximize Expected Net Realization (ENR)?_

For too long, agricultural tech has focused simply on displaying mandi (market) prices or basic truck-matching. Smart Krishi-Yatra AI challenges the assumption that the highest market price guarantees the highest income.

"The highest mandi price is not necessarily the highest farmer realization," said a spokesperson for Team Agnivega. "If a distant market offers a 15% higher price, but costs 20% more in transit fees and results in a 5% crop degradation due to time on the road, the farmer loses money by chasing the higher price. Our platform mathematically solves this."

### The Expected Net Realization (ENR) Innovation

At the core of Smart Krishi-Yatra AI is its proprietary ENR calculation and deterministic Capacity-Constrained Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP) engine. The system evaluates:

- **Market Price Prediction:** Utilizing historical data and local trends.
- **Transport Costs:** Matching loads with optimal fleet vehicles based on distance and capacity.
- **Time & Quality Degradation:** Factoring in the fragility of specific crops (e.g., tomatoes vs. potatoes) over projected transit times.
- **Risk Assessment:** Adjusting for weather and route reliability.

### Key Features of the Prototype:

- **Offline-First PWA:** Built to function seamlessly in low-connectivity rural areas using advanced local caching and background synchronization.
- **Voice IVR Prototype:** Allowing farmers to input load requirements naturally without complex text interfaces.
- **Dynamic Load Pooling:** Intelligently combining smaller LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) shipments from neighboring farms to maximize truck capacity and reduce costs.
- **Fleet Operator Cockpit:** Giving operators a birds-eye view of active shifts, driver compliance, and geo-fenced handovers.

### The Impact

By shifting the focus from gross market price to Net Realization, Smart Krishi-Yatra AI empowers farmers to make data-backed logistical decisions, reduces food waste through efficient routing, and lowers carbon emissions by minimizing empty truck miles.
