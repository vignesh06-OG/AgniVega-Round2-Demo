# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-11
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

Welcome to Smart Krishi-Yatra AI ("Agnivega", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our logistics platform, web application, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us

- **Account Information:** Name, phone number, role (Farmer, Fleet Operator, Driver), and vehicle details.
- **Logistics Data:** Crop types, payload weights, farm coordinates (pickup locations), and destination Mandi markets.
- **Voice Data:** If you use the Voice IVR interface, your voice audio is temporarily processed to extract logistics intent (e.g., "I have 5 tons of onions"). We do not persistently store raw audio recordings unless explicitly permitted.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

- **Location Data (GPS):** For drivers and fleet operators, we collect continuous background location data while a shift is active to provide real-time tracking, ETA calculations, and geofencing handovers.
- **Usage Data:** Device information, browser type, interaction logs, and offline synchronization telemetry.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Optimization:** To match farmers with the most efficient vehicle and calculate the Expected Net Realization (ENR).
- **Routing:** To generate capacity-constrained vehicle routing (CVRP) plans and navigation paths.
- **Communication:** To send SMS/WhatsApp alerts for job assignments and QR/Token handover receipts.
- **Service Improvement:** To train our deterministic algorithms and improve system reliability.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- **Offline-First Storage:** Smart Krishi-Yatra AI operates on a local-first PWA architecture. Much of your data is securely cached in your device's IndexedDB and synced to our servers only when a network connection is available.
- **Security Measures:** We use industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit and at rest. Handover tokens are cryptographically generated.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

- **Other Users:** Farmers see driver details and ETAs; Drivers see farm locations and load details.
- **Service Providers:** Third-party APIs (e.g., OpenRouteService, AI Gateways) used strictly for routing and transcription, bound by confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., DPDP Act in India), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can also withdraw consent for continuous location tracking by ending your shift or adjusting your device permissions.

## 6. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the Agnivega team at SmartKrishiYatra@noxverse.in.
